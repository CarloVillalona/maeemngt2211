    Grading

    A Midterm Grade covers Weeks 1–2 and a Pre-Final Grade covers Weeks 3–4. Each uses the same three components.

    20%Recitation, participation, oral reporting, assignments
    50%Special projects and research: the asynchronous tasks
    30%Major examination

    Final Grade = (Midterm Grade + Pre-Final Grade) ÷ 2. For MAEd students the lowest grade with credit is 2.00 (87–89%).

    Policies

    Communication

    Official communication (concerns, submissions, grade inquiries) goes through the professor’s email only:

    carlo.villalon@perpetualdalta.edu.ph

    The class Facebook group chat is for quick announcements and general engagement.

    Integrity and AI

    Submitted work must be your own analysis of your own institution, cited in APA 7th edition. AI tools may help you brainstorm, check language, or organize notes if you disclose the tool and its use at the end of the paper.

    Never enter learners’ or colleagues’ personal information into an AI tool or public website.

    Confidentiality

    When you write or present about your institution, remove the names of learners, colleagues, and parents, and do not share confidential records.
